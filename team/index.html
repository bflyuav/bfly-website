Our Journey

Team timeline

October 2025

Team Founded

Following a year of experience under a different name, BFL'Y was officially founded within Baykar Science High School. At the first meeting, the team's goals, sub-systems, and task assignments were defined, and work began right away.

February 2026

Delifişek's Design Completed

Inspired by the Akıncı, Delifişek's design was largely finalized. The aircraft's overall structure and technical specifications were determined, and a roadmap for production was set.

March 2026

Production Began, Applications Submitted

Production of the wing ribs marked the start of manufacturing. That same month, we completed our applications for the TEKNOFEST Combat UAV and SUAS competitions.

April 2026

Wing Production Completed

The wing production process for Delifişek was successfully completed, and the team moved on to fuselage production.

May 2026

Strong Result at KTR

In the report stage of the TEKNOFEST Combat UAV competition, known as KTR, we placed 7th out of 110 teams — a major achievement.

June – July 2026

First Flight and Test Flights

Delifişek's production was completed and the aircraft successfully completed its first flight. Throughout July, a series of test flights evaluated the aircraft's performance, and mission videos were submitted for both the SUAS and TEKNOFEST Combat UAV competitions.

October 2026

Starting Again

After reaching the semifinals in the 2026 season, we have started working toward the 2027 season.
